• Pro league. All names and data are fictional. Sporting (green) play Benfica (black), so the brand colours double as team colours.
• Scorekeepers log events on a courtside tablet (1b) or a phone (1c). Coaches and parents view the results on the other screens.
• The event model covers shots with location, OREB/DREB, AST, STL, BLK, TOV, fouls and subs. Every event has a clock time, player and team.
• Visuals: hardwood texture, green paint, Barlow Condensed for scores and Barlow for UI text.
